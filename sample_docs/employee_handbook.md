# Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Vacation Policy

New employees accrue 15 vacation days per year during their first two years
of employment. Starting in year three, accrual increases to 20 days per
year. Vacation days accrue monthly and unused days roll over up to a maximum
of 10 days into the following year.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every
six months.

## Onboarding

New hires complete a two-week onboarding program covering company systems,
security training, and team-specific shadowing. IT equipment is provisioned
before the employee's start date.

## Parental Leave

Employees are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave following the
birth, adoption, or fostering of a child, regardless of gender. Leave can be
taken in one continuous block or split into two blocks within the first 12
months.

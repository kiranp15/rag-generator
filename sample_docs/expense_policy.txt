Expense Reimbursement Policy

All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date
via the finance portal. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.

Meal reimbursement is capped at $50 per day for domestic travel and $75 per
day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any
circumstance.

Airfare must be booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business
class is permitted for flights over 6 hours with director approval.

Expense reports are typically reimbursed within 10 business days of
approval. Rejected expenses will include a reason and can be resubmitted
with additional documentation.
